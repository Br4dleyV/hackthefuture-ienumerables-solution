# Ilse Vermeer — Ilse’s Ledger

Source: Ilse’s HTF villager chat, as shared by the team. Ilse wants evidence she can explain to the council, not rumours.

## Suspect readings

- The bakery pipe’s sensor reports 140 L/min, its pipe record reports 47 L/min, and the pipe can carry only 50 L/min. The sensor reading cannot be right, but that does not establish that the pipe record is right. Ilse would not let Maren or Tobin act on either reading until someone checks it; Maren knows the pipe.
- Ilse gave no numerical tolerance for disagreement between otherwise plausible readings. She suggested asking Maren what variation is normal and left the threshold to the team. Any chosen rule must be explainable to the council in plain language.

## Audit evidence

- Ilse would inspect who last changed the affected record and when. She says every record keeps both details.
- Changes between midnight and 04:00, when nobody in the village works, or changes attributed to a system account or no name, concern her more than a faulty sensor. These are clues to investigate, not proof of tampering.
- A single nameless change at 02:15 is suspicious but not enough to take to the council. Ilse wants both a suspicious value (the new value is impossible **or** clashes with its sensor) **and** corroboration (other records were changed in the same odd way around that night).
- Ilse reads the audit trail; she referred questions about sensor faults to Maren.

## Still open

- How should the ledger group records changed “around that night”? Ilse did not specify an exact grouping window or a minimum number of matching records.
- Which account names are system accounts, which timestamps represent village local time, and do changes at exactly midnight or 04:00 count?
